What is Chemistry?

Chapter 1 · Lesson 1 of 7 🧪 Chemistry ⏱ 10–15 min read

🎯 Why Should You Care?

Right now, take a quick look around your room. You see: the air you breathe, a glass of water, your phone (glass + plastic), chai, salt, petrol in motorbikes outside.

Here's the magical truth: everything you just looked at is made of tiny invisible particles, and the study of these particles is called Chemistry. Even you are a walking chemistry experiment — your body is doing thousands of chemical reactions every second right now!

📖 Core Concept: What is Chemistry?

📚 Textbook Definition

Chemistry is the branch of science that deals with the composition, structure, properties, and changes of matter.

✅ Easy Version

Chemistry is the science of "what things are made of" and "how they change into other things."

Breaking Down the 4 Key Words

WordWhat it meansExample
Composition What is it made of? Water = hydrogen + oxygen
Structure How are pieces arranged? Diamond and pencil lead are both carbon — just arranged differently
Properties How does it behave? Salt dissolves in water; oil does not
Changes How does it turn into something else? Wood burning → ash + smoke

What is Matter?

📚 Definition

Matter = anything that has mass AND takes up space (volume).

        MATTER
          |
    ┌─────┴─────┐
    |           |
  Has mass    Takes space
  (weight)    (volume)

Quick Test: Is It Matter?

  • Air → has mass + takes up space → ✅ Matter
  • A thought → no mass, no space → ❌ Not matter
  • Light → no mass → ❌ Not matter (it's energy)
  • School bag → has mass + takes space → ✅ Matter
  • Water → ✅ Matter

💡 Chemistry All Around You

You don't need a lab coat — chemistry is happening everywhere right now:

  1. Cooking 🍳 — Frying an egg is a chemical change. The egg protein changes permanently — you can't un-fry it.
  2. Rusting 🔩 — That brown layer on an iron gate? Iron reacting with oxygen and water vapor in air.
  3. Photosynthesis 🌱 — Plants use sunlight to convert CO₂ + water → sugar + oxygen. Chemistry keeping plants alive.
  4. Medicine 💊 — Panadol works because of chemistry: it blocks the enzyme (chemical) that causes pain signals.
  5. Battery 🔋 — Your phone is powered by chemical reactions inside the lithium-ion battery.
  6. Soap 🧼 — Soap molecules have one end attracted to oil and another to water — that's why they clean grease off hands.

⚠️ Common Confusions

⚠️ Confusion #1: "Is air really matter? It feels like nothing!"

✅ Yes, air is matter! Proof: take an empty balloon and weigh it. Now inflate it and weigh again — the inflated balloon is heavier because air has mass.

⚠️ Confusion #2: "Chemistry = just memorizing formulas"

❌ Wrong! Chemistry is mostly about understanding why. Once you understand why something happens, formulas come naturally. Don't memorize blindly — understand first.

⚠️ Confusion #3: "Organic = healthy/natural (like organic vegetables)"

❌ In chemistry, "organic" means containing carbon atoms. Nothing to do with farming! Petrol and plastic are also "organic" in chemistry terms.

Bonus: Why Light is NOT Matter

Light is made of photons which have zero rest mass. It's energy, not matter. Here's a comparison:

PropertyStone 🪨Light 💡
Can you weigh it?✅ Yes❌ No
Takes up space?✅ Yes❌ No
Has mass?✅ Yes❌ No
Is it matter?✅ Yes❌ No — it's energy
Key Takeaway

Universe = Matter (stone, water, air, you — has mass) + Energy (light, heat, sound, electricity — no mass). Chemistry studies matter and how energy changes it.

Q4. Define matter and give two examples.

Answer: Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space (volume).
Examples: water (liquid matter), air (gaseous matter).

Q5. Name any three branches of chemistry and write one line about each.

Answer:
1. Organic Chemistry — studies carbon-containing compounds like petrol and sugar.
2. Inorganic Chemistry — studies compounds that do not contain carbon, like salt and metals.
3. Biochemistry — studies the chemical reactions that happen inside living organisms.

Q6. Is light matter? Give a reason.

Answer: No, light is not matter. Matter must have mass and occupy space. Light (photons) has zero rest mass and does not occupy space — it is a form of energy.

📝 Summary

3 Key Points from This Lesson

  1. Chemistry = the study of what things are made of and how they change (composition, structure, properties, changes of matter).
  2. Matter = anything with mass + volume. Air, water, and you are matter. Light and heat are energy — NOT matter.
  3. Chemistry has 7 main branches: Physical, Organic, Inorganic, Biochemistry, Analytical, Nuclear, Industrial — each studying a different area.
